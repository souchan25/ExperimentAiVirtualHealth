# CPSU AI Virtual Health Assistant - Current Platform Features

## 🎓 Student Side
- **AI-Guided Consultations:** Real-time chat with AI to assess symptoms and receive immediate health guidance.
- **Smart Triage System:** Automatic classification (Low/Moderate/High/Emergency) based on reported symptoms with NLP parsing.
- **Floating SOS Emergency Button:** One-tap emergency trigger that alerts campus clinic staff instantly with your location.
- **Digital Pillbox:** Centralized view of all active medications and prescriptions issued by university clinic staff.
* **Medical Excuse Slip Automation:** Request and download digitally generated excuse slips after staff approval.
- **Medical Records AI:** Upload medical documents (lab results, certificates) for automated AI clinical data extraction.
- **Wellness Check-ins:** Periodic mental and physical health tracking (Mood, Stress, and Physical status).
- **First-Aid Knowledge Base:** Searchable repository of verified first-aid and self-care protocols.
- **Appointment Booking:** Schedule and manage in-person or telehealth visits directly from the dashboard.
- **Direct Messaging:** Secure 1-on-1 messaging channel with clinic staff for follow-up guidance.
- **Campus Health Alerts:** Live banner notifications for university-wide health updates (Info/Warning/Critical).
- **Offline Symptom Logging:** Local caching and automatic syncing of symptom reports to the database when connection is restored.
- **Personal Health Profile:** Manage medical data, including blood type, allergies, and pre-existing conditions.
- **Consultation History:** Accessible archives of all past AI and staff interactions.
- **Privacy & Consent:** Integrated consent management for medical data handling.

## 🏥 Clinic Staff Side
- **SOS Emergency Monitor:** Real-time tracking of student SOS triggers for immediate medical response.
- **Clinical Case Management:** Review pending consultations, triage levels, and detailed student symptom summaries.
- **AI-Human Collaborative Review:** Access full student-AI chat logs with the ability to override/assign triage levels and add clinical notes.
- **Pharmacy & Inventory Management:** Track medical stock, manage inventory items, and receive low-stock alerts.
- **Geospatial Hotspot Mapping:** Visualize health trends and clusters on a campus map based on reported symptoms.
- **Advanced Health Audit Reports:** Generate university-wide analytics in PDF (branded) and Excel formats.
- **Anonymized DOH Export:** Export epidemiological data stripped of PII for public health reporting (DOH standards).
- **Health Alert Broadcaster:** Create and push campus-wide health alerts with specific severity levels.
- **Appointment Terminal:** Manage and oversee the university's medical appointment schedule.

## ⚙️ Admin (System Oversight)
- **Staff Provisioning:** Exclusive tool for creating and managing secure Clinic Staff and Senior Staff accounts.
- **Full User Registry:** Oversee all students and staff on the platform with account management capabilities.
- **Immutable Audit Logs:** Non-editable security logs recording all system actions and resource access.
- **System Backups:** Manual trigger functionality for database and document backups.
- **Admin Analytics:** Real-time university engagement statistics and flagged case overview.

## 🧠 AI Engine & Security
- **Clinical NLP Extraction:** Automated extraction of symptoms and durations using OpenAI Vision/LLM.
- **Structured Output Integrity:** AI engine enforced to return consistent triage and conditions format.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Granular permissions ensuring data privacy between students, staff, and admins.
- **Encrypted Data Storage:** Security-first approach for all sensitive health records.
